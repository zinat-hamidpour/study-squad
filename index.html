THE CLIMB

How the climb works

At 4 AM we all stand on the same step. Reach your own goal for the day → you climb that day's step.

Didn't reach it? At 4 AM a faint, unlit lantern takes your place on that step and you move up with everyone.

A step lights up when at least 3 of us climbed it.

Study double your goal one day → you earn a lantern 🏮 (it hangs at your seat in the study hall). Hang it on a friend's unlit lantern from the last 2 days to light it. Each person can get one lantern a week.

On Friday we count the unlit lanterns nobody lit: 3 or fewer → Friday Rooftop for all of us + a gold cup on the shelf. 4 to 6 → a silver cup. More → no cup.
